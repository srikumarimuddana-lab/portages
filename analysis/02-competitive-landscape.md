# 02 — Does Portage Already Exist? Canadian Competitive Landscape

> Synthesis of `research/research_1_canada.md` (≈28 web searches) and `research/research_2_regina.md`. All claims carry source links there; key sources repeated inline. Research date: 2026-08-28.

## Verdict

**No single company is Portage — but every component exists somewhere, and the graveyard is large.**

As of August 2026 there is no Canadian platform that is simultaneously (a) owner-direct for **both sales and rentals**, (b) genuinely **zero-fee for everyone**, (c) **AI-native** in search and listing creation, and (d) **present in Regina**. The space is *open*, not occupied.

But the risk is not competition — **it is the business model**. Every well-funded Canadian attempt to attack commissions between 2022–2023 died or was gutted.

## The graveyard — read this twice

| Company | Money in | Outcome | Cause of death |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Purplebricks Canada → FairSquare** | Desjardins paid **$60.5M** (2020) | **Shut down Feb 2023** | Fixed fee (~$3,499 upfront) + collapsing volume + salaried-agent overhead. Desjardins: "the rapid deterioration of the housing market and its business model do not allow us to continue" |
| **Properly** | Large VC raise | Sold to Compass for **$32M equity**, founders exited (Sept 2023) | Balance-sheet guarantees died in the downturn |
| **Unreserved** | **$33.85M seed** (2022) | Auctions paused 2023; staff 150→70; current status unverified | Fought CREA/OREA/OREB with a $25M lawsuit, then dropped it |
| **Homie** (US, Utah) | ~$35M | Dead by Apr 2024; antitrust appeal dismissed Aug 2026 | Flat $3,500 fee, rate-shock volume collapse, alleged agent steering |
| **ForSaleByOwner.com** (US) | 20 years of operation | Sold for **$2.5M** (2018) | FSBO-only positioning has a tiny ceiling |

**The single autopsy line they share:** *fixed or falling revenue per listing, against cyclical transaction volume, carrying human-service costs.*

Portage's "free forever" removes the revenue lever entirely — which is survivable **only** if marginal cost per listing is near zero (AI self-serve, no salaried agents) and revenue comes from adjacencies. That is precisely the design intent, and it must be defended ruthlessly.

## Rental platforms — where the real Regina gap is

| Platform | Landlord cost | Regina? | Weakness Portage attacks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RentFaster.ca** | **$54.50 + tax per listing** | **Yes — market leader in Regina** | Paid, Calgary-centric tooling, no AI, no verification depth |
| **Rentals.ca** | Free first 3 listings, then subscription | Yes | Syndication network, thin trust features |
| **Kijiji** | Free for individuals | Yes (~350–416 live Regina ads) | **Classifieds only — no verification, no applications, no bookings** |
| **Facebook Marketplace / Regina FB groups** | Free | Yes, heavily used | **No verification at all — the primary scam vector** |
| **liv.rent** | Free tier → $56/mo → $490/mo; Equifax-backed Trust Score | **No Regina presence** | The most complete trust/transaction rails in Canada — and absent from your market |
| **Zumper** | Free <5 properties | Yes (market pages) | Thin Canadian feature depth |

**The gap in one sentence:** nobody offers liv.rent-grade verification in Regina; RentFaster charges for listings; and the free tier (Kijiji + Facebook) is where the fraud lives.

## Why the trust wedge is real, with numbers

- The **FCAA (Saskatchewan), the Saskatchewan REALTORS® Association, the Saskatchewan Landlord Association and BBB Saskatchewan** have all issued fake-listing scam warnings — scammers impersonate landlords and demand e-transfer deposits before viewings.
- Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre: **108,878 fraud reports, $638M reported losses in 2024**; only **5–10% of fraud is ever reported**.
- US FTC: about **half of rental-scam reports** in the 12 months to June 2025 started with a **fake Facebook ad**.
- Victims skew to **newcomers and students renting sight-unseen** — exactly Regina's growth demographics.

**Implication:** market *"verified owners, zero scams"* harder than *"zero fees"* on the rental side. Free is not differentiating in rentals (Kijiji and Facebook are already free); **verified is.**

## Ten takeaways

1. **The bundle is novel; no component is.** Portage's novelty is the combination + zero price + Regina focus.
2. **Bōde is the benchmark to beat** — already in SK, $949 or 1% capped, with MLS access you won't have.
3. **AVM is table stakes** (HonestDoor gives it free nationally). Differentiate on Regina open-data depth, not existence.
4. **AI chat search will be commoditized** — Realtor.com shipped natural-language search in Oct 2025 and RealAssist AI in June 2026; Zillow launched a ChatGPT app in Oct 2025. Speed matters; it is not a moat.
5. **Trust/verification is the defensible feature**, backed by documented Saskatchewan scam warnings.
6. **Rentals are the wedge** — 2× the volume, weekly engagement, and the incumbents are weakest there.
7. **Saskatchewan is genuinely underserved** — HouseSigma absent, liv.rent absent, Point2 exited, Wahi/Bōde nominal.
8. **MLS access is the strategic fork.** Only brokerages touch MLS®; mere postings cost $500–$1,500. Off-MLS generalists outside Quebec have always starved for buyer traffic.
9. **Incumbent risk is rising:** REALTOR.ca became a for-profit subsidiary (Jan 2025) under an **ex-Kijiji CEO** (Jan 2026) — expect faster feature copying.
10. **Regulatory tailwind:** the Competition Bureau is actively investigating CREA's commission rules and expanded to Greater Vancouver in Jan 2026 — expressly probing whether policy "makes it more difficult for **alternative listing services** to compete." That is you.
