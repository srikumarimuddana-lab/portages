# 03 — What to Learn from the US and India

> Synthesis of `research/research_3_us.md` and `research/research_4_india.md`. Full sources and figures in those files. Research date: 2026-08-28.

## Part A — United States

### The single most important structural insight

**Zillow deliberately handicaps owner-direct supply.** FSBO listings are free to post but are **excluded from the default "Agent listings" tab** and buried under "By owner & other," which many buyers never open. Zillow cannot fix this without destroying Premier Agent revenue (agents pay the bills).

> **A marketplace whose *default tab* is owner listings is structurally differentiated — and the incumbent cannot copy it.** That is Portage's real wedge, not AI.

Homes.com made the agent-world version of this promise — **"Your Listing, Your Lead"** (leads go free to the listing agent, never resold) — and grew brand awareness from 4% → 33% in a year. For Portage the owner *is* the listing agent, so the promise is native and costs nothing.

### How the US actually monetizes (the ladder)

| Rank | Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tenant-paid rental application** | Zillow: **$35 once → unlimited applications for 30 days** (credit + background + eviction), free for landlords. TurboTenant/Avail: $45–55/applicant. TurboTenant calls application fees its primary revenue. ⚠️ **Blocked in Saskatchewan — see doc 05** |
| 2 | **Payment processing spread** | Card 2.95–3.49% tenant-paid; ACH $1–2 or free as a subscription carrot |
| 3 | **Landlord subscription** | TurboTenant **$149–199/yr**; Avail $9/unit/mo — the sweet spot |
| 4 | **Rent reporting to credit bureaus** | RentRedi/TurboTenant **$5.99/mo, tenant-paid** — tenants volunteer |
| 5 | **Insurance attach** | Renters insurance at lease signing, home insurance at purchase — commission, zero user fee |
| 6 | **Premium listing media** | **Zillow Showcase**: ~$30–80/listing; listings sell for **~$7,000 more**, +75% views, +68% saves; agents win 30% more listings |
| 7 | **Agent lead-gen** | Zillow Premier Agent: ~$20–60/lead (up to $450+ in hot metros), or **Flex at a 15–40% referral fee** on closed commissions |
| 8 | **Mortgage attach** | Zillow Home Loans: **~44% attach** in enhanced markets; BuyAbility affordability tool has **2.9M enrollees**; Q2 2026 originations $2.2B (+95%) |

**Zillow FY2025: $2.6B revenue (+16%). Rentals grew fastest: +45% YoY.** That is the tell — rentals, not resale, are the growth engine even for the biggest resale portal in the world.

### Failure lessons (what NOT to do)

1. **Never take balance-sheet risk on your own AVM.** Zillow Offers lost **>$1B in 3.5 years** and died in one quarter of market turn. Opendoor lost **$1.3B in 2025** even as the surviving category leader.
2. **Never sell one lead to five pros.** Thumbtack: 1,000+ BBB complaints, chronic pro churn. Angi deliberately destroyed **81% of its network lead volume** to escape the model — and pro churn fell ~30%, retention +17%.
3. **Flat-fee human brokerage does not survive downturns.** Homie ($35M raised) dead by 2024. Redfin abandoned salaried agents in 2024, then sold to Rocket Companies for $1.75B (closed July 2025) — valued as **mortgage top-of-funnel, not as a brokerage**.
4. **Don't bet on courts or commission collapse.** Post-NAR-settlement buyer-agent commissions moved **2.36% → 2.40%**. Homie's antitrust suit was dismissed; the appeal was quashed in Aug 2026.
5. **You cannot outspend your way to revenue.** Homes.com ran a Super Bowl campaign, hit 100M+ monthly uniques — and still sits at ~$100M run-rate vs Realtor.com's $610M. **Win one city with liquidity, not national ads.**
6. **Don't run a second brand** — Trulia's neighbourhood-UGC treasure decayed under Zillow's shadow. One brand, all features.
7. **Free-forever with no monetization plan is also fatal** — Azibo (free, banking-monetized) was absorbed by TurboTenant in May 2025.

## Part B — India (NoBroker & MyGate)

### NoBroker — the closest thing to Portage's thesis, proven at scale

Founded 2013-14 in Bengaluru on exactly your premise: **connect owners and tenants directly, cut the broker**. Brokers are **contractually prohibited** from listing, so "zero brokerage" is architecturally enforced, not a marketing claim.

**Scale & money:** ~**$366M raised**; unicorn Nov 2021 at $1B. FY24 operating revenue **₹803 Cr (+32%)** — but a **net loss of ₹411 Cr**, with a criticized 57% of expenses in unexplained overheads. March 2026: reportedly >100k real-estate transactions and ~150k home-services orders in the month.

**How the free model pays for itself:**
1. Free listings + limited free contacts → overwhelming owner supply
2. Urgency monetizes the *demand* side (plans ₹1.6k–6k vs a broker's ₹15k–50k)
3. **Guarantee tiers** ("MoneyBack": refund if no home/tenant found) monetize risk-aversion at 2–6× base price
4. **Transaction-moment attach**: everyone renting needs an agreement, movers, cleaning
5. **NoBrokerHood** (society app: 25,000+ societies, ~4–4.8M families) locks in the community layer where tomorrow's listings originate

**The AI lesson worth stealing:** NoBroker's **ConvoZen** conversational AI is trained on 45,000+ hours of its own calls, claims **100% audit coverage** (vs ~2% human sampling), and **auto-delists a property in real time** when it detects the property is already rented. Pure software; no labour cost.

**Their weaknesses (the anti-patterns):** imposter listings that passed verification; recurring **MoneyBack refund disputes** on technicalities; aggressive telesales citing inflated lead counts; broker infiltration; opaque cost structure.

### MyGate — the surprise finding

Community/visitor-management app: **27,000+ societies, 50+ cities, ~5.7M homes**. FY25 revenue ₹173.5 Cr (+80%), **EBITDA-positive**; FY26 ₹232 Cr.

**~65–70% of its income is advertising, not SaaS** — selling brands hyperlocal access to verified, affluent, address-confirmed households at postal-code granularity.

**What translates to Canada:** not the gate. Canadian condos already have professional managers and entrenched software (Condo Control, FrontSteps), and Regina has almost no staffed/gated buildings or domestic-staff labour market. What survives is: **(a)** a verified local resident audience is worth more than SaaS fees, **(b)** community software sees **move-outs before the market does** — a vacancy pipeline, and **(c)** ratings as trust infrastructure.

### The cautionary tale: NestAway

Managed-rental startup, ~₹700 Cr raised (Tiger Global, Ratan Tata), valued ₹1,800 Cr in 2020 — **sold for ₹90 Cr in 2023, a ~95% haircut.** Cause: wafer-thin margins on full-stack rental management, COVID vacancy shock, and destroyed trust on both sides (owners blamed damage/vacancies, tenants fought over deposit refunds).

> **Rule: be the marketplace and tools layer. Never become the counterparty holding deposits or guaranteeing rent.**

Similarly **Stanza Living** (75,000+ co-living beds) turned its first profit in FY25 only via ₹278 Cr of *non-operating* income. **List rooms; don't operate them.**

## What does NOT translate from India

| Feature | Why it fails in Regina |
|---|---|
| Contact paywalls at launch | Facebook Marketplace is free and liquid here; gating contacts kills the network before it forms. NoBroker earned this only after becoming the default supply |
| Guard app / biometric gate entry / visitor OTPs | No gated-society culture, no cheap 24/7 guards; Condo Control owns the Canadian condo niche |
| Domestic-staff attendance & maid discovery | The underlying daily-help labour market doesn't exist in Canada |
| "Police verification" of tenants | Non-compliant and alienating; consent-based credit/background checks are the lawful equivalent |
| Doorstep document delivery / e-stamping | No stamp duty on Saskatchewan leases; solves a problem Canadians don't have |
| Aggregator-branded movers with platform-set pricing | Canadian labour costs + liability make it uneconomic |
| Cheap human "Relationship Managers" | Built on Indian labour arbitrage; at Canadian wages this is a loss machine — and it is NoBroker's #1 complaint source anyway |
| WhatsApp-first engagement | Canada runs on SMS/email/push |
| Full-stack managed rentals holding deposits | NestAway. See above |
| Ads as launch revenue | MyGate's ad engine needs 5.7M dense households; Regina can't support it for years |
