## 1. Does a company like Portage already exist? Company-by-company

### 1.1 Realtor.ca / REALTOR.ca Canada Inc. (CREA) — the incumbent utility

### 1.4 PropertyGuys.com (Moncton) — the FSBO survivor

- **Status:** Alive; franchise network (search results show active franchise recruitment through 2026) ([franchisehelp.com](https://www.franchisehelp.com/franchises/propertyguyscom/), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Model & pricing:** customized FSBO packages at a **one-time flat fee of roughly $2,000–$9,000 depending on market/package, no commission**; franchisees pay $20K–$40K initial fee + 8% royalties ([sharpsheets.io FDD analysis](https://sharpsheets.io/blog/propertyguys-com-franchise-fdd-profits-costs/), 2025; [franchiseoverview.com](https://franchiseoverview.com/company/propertyguys), 2026).
- **Scale:** listing counts not published (**UNVERIFIED**); long-lived (founded late 1990s in Moncton — widely reported; exact-date source not captured this pass, **UNVERIFIED**).
- **Strengths/weaknesses:** durable through cycles because local franchisees carry costs; but expensive vs Bōde/ComFree, dated tech, no AI, and the franchise structure makes national product velocity slow.

### 1.5 Purplebricks Canada → FairSquare — the cautionary tale

Timeline (all sourced):

1. Purplebricks (UK) entered Canada 2018 by buying **DuProprio/ComFree for ~CAD $51M** ([Purplebricks investor PDF](https://pbinvestmedia.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/presentation/media_url/100/Acquisition_of_Canadian_digital_real_estate_service_DuProprio-ComFree.pdf), 2018; [cbc.ca on the Canada entry](https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.4787422), 2018).
2. Fee model: **flat fee ~$3,499 upfront "regardless of if your home sells"** (comparison page: [home.bode.ca/how-bode-compares-to-purplebricks](https://home.bode.ca/how-bode-compares-to-purplebricks/), accessed 2026-08-28); an $800 entry offer existed in Alberta c. 2019 ([cbc.ca Edmonton](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/edmonton/real-estate-edmonton-technology-1.5350349), 2019).
3. Purplebricks exited Canada: **Desjardins paid $60.5M in mid-2020** for the Canadian subsidiary (incl. DuProprio) ([theglobeandmail.com](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-desjardins-group-shuts-down-fairsquare-realty-blames-slow-housing/), Feb 2023).
4. Rebranded **FairSquare** January 2022 ([estateagenttoday.co.uk](https://www.estateagenttoday.co.uk/breaking-news/2022/01/goodbye-purplebricks-name-disappears-in-rebrand-of-canada-firm/), Jan 2022).
5. **Shut down 2023-02-16** ("no longer accepting new business") ([realestatemagazine.ca](https://realestatemagazine.ca/fairsquare-no-longer-accepting-new-business/), Feb 2023; [storeys.com](https://storeys.com/fairsquare-realty-shut-down-desjardins/), Feb 2023). DuProprio was kept alive.

**Why it failed (post-mortem):** Desjardins' spokesperson: "the rapid deterioration of the housing market and its business model do not allow us to continue operations" — a **fixed-fee model needs high transaction volume**, and volume collapsed (January 2023 national sales hit a 14-year low) ([theglobeandmail.com](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-desjardins-group-shuts-down-fairsquare-realty-blames-slow-housing/), Feb 2023; [storeys.com](https://storeys.com/fairsquare-realty-shut-down-desjardins/), Feb 2023). Analytically: it charged **upfront** fees (seller pays even if no sale — reputational drag), carried salaried-agent overhead unlike a pure marketplace, and lacked Quebec-style cultural tailwinds outside Quebec. **Lesson for Portage:** revenue tied to listing volume in a cyclical market killed a player with a $60M corporate parent; "free for everyone" removes that revenue lever entirely, so Portage must be structurally cheap to run and monetize adjacencies (services, not transactions).

### 1.6 HouseSigma — data/AI leader, agent-based model

- **Status:** Alive; **2M+ monthly users**, 4.9/5 Trustpilot; operates as a VOW brokerage ([spark.mwm.ai app profile](https://spark.mwm.ai/en/apps/housesigma-canada-real-estate/1255490256), accessed 2026-08-28; [ca.trustpilot.com](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/housesigma.com), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Model:** free app (sold prices, listing history, **AI valuation**, alerts, school/neighbourhood data, rental-yield tools); monetizes as a brokerage (agents/referrals) ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/housesigma-canada-real-estate/id1255490256), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Coverage:** **Ontario, BC, Alberta only** — FAQ says more provinces "no ETA"; **Saskatchewan not covered** ([housesigma.com FAQ](https://housesigma.com/blog-en/faq/looking-for-properties/will-you-expand-to-other-provinces/), accessed 2026-08-28; [hardbacon.ca review](https://hardbacon.ca/en/mortgage/housesigma-review/), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Relevance to Portage:** proves Canadians flock to free data/AI tools; but it's a buyer-research layer on the agent system, not owner-direct — and it leaves SK open.

### 1.7 Zolo — portal + brokerage hybrid

- **Status:** Alive. Claims **10M+ monthly home shoppers** and **~150,000 live listings**, offices across Canada ([zolo.ca/about](https://www.zolo.ca/about.php), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Model:** commission brokerage (splits with "commission-only" agents; ~$11K gross commission per sale per a business-model profile) + advertising/premium placements for agents ([vizologi profile](https://vizologi.com/business-strategy-canvas/zolo-business-model-canvas/), accessed 2026-08-28). - **Coverage:** national incl. Saskatchewan ([zolo.ca/saskatchewan-real-estate](https://www.zolo.ca/saskatchewan-real-estate), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Relevance:** SEO powerhouse for MLS search; not owner-direct, no zero-fee angle.

### 1.8 Point2 / Point2Homes (Saskatoon-born) — pivoted away from for-sale

- **History:** founded **1996 in Saskatoon** (originally heavy-equipment marketplace), pivoted to real estate 2002, launched Point2Homes 2004; **acquired by Yardi Systems in 2010** ([yardi.com](https://www.yardi.com/news/press-releases/yardi-systems-acquires-point2-technologies-inc/), 2010; [landlordtalking.com](https://www.landlordtalking.com/tips/saskatoons-point2-technologies-sold-to-u-s-company/), 2010; [point2homes.com/AboutUs](https://www.point2homes.com/AboutUs), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Current status (major change):** Point2Homes now "**specializes exclusively in houses for rent** in the U.S. and Canada" as part of Yardi's Rental Network (with RentCafe); it **stopped accepting new manual for-sale listings and ad purchases on 2024-05-15** and phased out MLS feed listings ([point2homes.com/AboutUs](https://www.point2homes.com/AboutUs), accessed 2026-08-28; [abovemedia.ca](https://abovemedia.ca/blog/point2-alternative-for-real-estate-marketing-2024/), 2024; [ushombi.com](https://www.ushombi.com/2024/08/07/point2-caribbean-real-estate-operations/), Aug 2024).
- **Relevance:** Saskatchewan's most famous proptech is gone from the for-sale market — a symbolic gap Portage could claim, and a reminder that horizontal portals get absorbed by rental-ad economics.

### 1.10 HonestDoor (Edmonton) — free AVM + $500 mere posting

- **Status:** Alive (city pages current to Aug 2026) ([honestdoor.com Edmonton](https://www.honestdoor.com/cities/ab/edmonton), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Model:** free **HonestDoor Price** AVM for residential properties across Canada (launched nationally June 2022) ([businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220622005039/en/HonestDoor-Launches-Free-Home-Valuations-for-Residential-Properties-Across-Canada), Jun 2022); sells data products; and offers a **$500 FSBO listing onto REALTOR.ca/MLS®** ([businesswire.com, same release], 2022; corroborated by [honestdoor.wheree.com](https://honestdoor.wheree.com/) reviews page, accessed 2026-08-28 — current price **UNVERIFIED**, may have changed).

### 1.11 Properly — pivoted, sold for parts (2023)

- Raised big, offered "buy-before-you-sell" guarantee; **dropped the purchase guarantee Jan 2023**; **laid off 71** in 2022 ([theglobeandmail.com](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/real-estate/article-real-estate-startup-properly-chops-71-jobs/), 2022); **acquired by Compass (US) Sept 2023 at $32M equity value**, with the brokerage/search portal sold separately; founders exited ([betakit.com](https://betakit.com/properly-founders-exit-as-company-and-tech-are-sold-in-separate-transactions/), Sep 2023; [ncfacanada.org](https://ncfacanada.org/properly-gets-acquired-for-32-million-and-founders-exit/), 2023).
- **Lesson:** balance-sheet-heavy models (guarantees/iBuying) die in Canadian downturns; capital-light marketplace survives longer.

### 1.12 Unreserved — auctions blocked by industry, then faded

- Ottawa-based; raised a **$33.85M seed (Jan 2022)**, one of Canada's largest ([businesswire.com](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220113005330/en/Canadian-Real-Estate-Innovator-Unreserved-Inc.-Raises-33.85-Million-Seed-Round), Jan 2022; [betakit.com](https://betakit.com/unreserved-closes-33-million-seed-round-to-build-property-auction-platform/), 2022).
- Fought CREA/OREA/OREB with a **$25M lawsuit** alleging the industry tried to shut it down; later dropped ([cbc.ca](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/ottawa/unreserved-real-estate-lawsuit-oreb-orea-crea-1.6539915), 2022; [betakit.com](https://betakit.com/unreserved-dismisses-portion-of-25-million-lawsuit-against-real-estate-associations-as-new-regulations-for-open-bidding-expected/), 2023).
- **Paused auctions April 2023** (staff cut from ~150 to ~70), continued as a conventional brokerage; acquired brokerage BMC Casa (Georgetown, ON) Nov 2023 ([betakit.com](https://betakit.com/unreserved-dismisses-portion-of-25-million-lawsuit-against-real-estate-associations-as-new-regulations-for-open-bidding-expected/), Apr 2023). **Current 2025-26 operating status: UNVERIFIED** — no recent coverage found; PitchBook/CB Insights profiles list it but confirm nothing current ([pitchbook.com](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/490044-97), accessed 2026-08-28).
- **Lesson:** consumer appetite for transaction-model innovation exists, but organized-realty friction (board access, data, lawsuits) is a real, expensive risk for anyone touching the transaction.

## 3. FSBO & flat-fee history and economics in Canada

**Who tried / who died / who survived:**

- **Died or absorbed:** ComFree outside Quebec (folded into Purplebricks then FairSquare, closed 2023); Purplebricks Canada/FairSquare (2023, §1.5); Properly (sold for parts, 2023, §1.11); Unreserved's auction model (paused 2023, §1.12); Redfin's 1%-listing push never scaled here (§4); Point2Homes exited for-sale entirely (2024, §1.8).
- **Survived:** DuProprio (Quebec, flat-fee, corporate parent Desjardins); PropertyGuys (franchise flat-fee); Bōde (success-fee 1% capped); HonestDoor (data + cheap mere posting); One-off flat-fee brokerages (e.g., $1,199 mere postings — §6); saskhouses.com-type classifieds.
- **Reborn:** **ComFree relaunched in 2024** by co-founder Travis Holowach as a national flat-fee MLS service — "the Wealthsimple of real estate" — packages **$497 to $1,197** ($997+GST flagship incl. MLS/REALTOR.ca listing, photos/virtual tour, signage, pricing support, offer forms, discounted legal) ([theglobeandmail.com](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/personal-finance/article-homesales-without-realtor-comfree-turbotax-business/), accessed 2026-08-28; [comfree.com/pricing](https://comfree.com/pricing/), accessed 2026-08-28; [relaunch post](https://comfree.com/real-estate-disruptor-comfree-is-back/), 2024).

**FSBO share of Canadian sales:** CREA has historically said **75–80% of Canadian homes sell through REALTORS®**, implying a ~20-25% non-broker share including private sales and mere postings ([realestatemagazine.ca](https://realestatemagazine.ca/putting-for-sale-by-owner-into-perspective/), accessed 2026-08-28 — older CREA figure; precise current national FSBO% is **not published/UNVERIFIED**). Regionally it is wildly uneven: GTA FSBO was estimated at **~1% of sales** (2021) ([insightt.ca](https://www.insightt.ca/blog/the-for-sale-by-owner-debate-put-into-perspective), 2021), while **Quebec** private sales are mainstream (DuProprio alone ~20% in 2018 — §1.3). For calibration, US FSBO share fell to ~5% by 2025 ([rubyhome.com FSBO stats](https://www.rubyhome.com/blog/fsbo-stats/), accessed 2026-08-28).

**Why DuProprio worked in Quebec specifically** (facts + analysis):
1. **Civil-law conveyancing via notaries** — every Quebec transaction already requires a notary, so removing the agent doesn't remove legal safety; DuProprio bundles notary phone support ([duproprio.com pricing snippet], accessed 2026-08-28).
2. **Full-stack support, not a bare classifieds site** — photographers, appraisers, coaches, negotiation guidance ([espaceproprio.com](https://www.espaceproprio.com/en/article/duproprio-selling-on-your-own), accessed 2026-08-28).
3. **25+ years of brand & density** — founded 1997, enough listing density that buyers routinely check it alongside Centris (analysis; density figure UNVERIFIED).
4. **Distinct market** — French-language marketing moat and a provincial MLS (Centris) separate from REALTOR.ca reduced the "must be on national MLS" pull (analysis).
5. **Deep-pocketed parents** (Yellow Pages → Purplebricks → Desjardins) funded it through cycles — the same cycles that killed stand-alone fixed-fee players (§1.5).

**Economic lesson:** every Canadian fixed-fee/FSBO death shares one autopsy line — *fixed revenue per listing versus volume-cyclical demand plus heavy human-service costs*. Survivors either cap human service (mere postings, classifieds), charge on success with a cap (Bōde), franchise the cost base (PropertyGuys), or have a parent (DuProprio). A **zero-fee** model escapes the cyclicality trap only if marginal cost per listing is near zero (AI-heavy self-serve) and revenue comes from adjacent services — which is precisely Portage's stated design (home-services marketplace), but note that BetaKit reports investors in 2025-26 now *favour* recurring-revenue software "over the marketplace and transaction-fee structures that defined the last cycle" ([betakit.com](https://betakit.com/how-ai-and-a-tight-fundraising-market-are-reshaping-canadian-proptech/), 2025/2026).

## 4. Zillow and Redfin in Canada (2026 status)

**Zillow:** entered Canada in 2018 via brokerage feed partnerships (first ~50,000 listings) ([cbc.ca](https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/zillow-ap-website-canadian-homes-real-estate-listings-1.4864964), 2018); grew past **250 Canadian brokerage/franchisor partners by Aug 2019** ([newswire.ca](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/zillow-now-partners-with-over-250-canadian-brokerages-and-franchisors-886620970.html), 2019). It still maintains a Canadian industry-partnership program (page updated Jan 2025) ([zillow.com/c/canada](https://www.zillow.com/c/canada/), as of Jan 2025) and is a syndication target for Bōde and ComFree listings ([home.bode.ca](https://home.bode.ca/for-sale-by-owner/), accessed 2026-08-28). **No Canadian Zestimate, no Canadian rentals marketplace, no boots on the ground; no 2024-26 expansion news found** — Canada remains a partner-fed display market for Zillow (absence of news **UNVERIFIED** as a definitive statement of strategy).

**Redfin:** launched Canada in **2019** (Toronto, then Vancouver) with salaried agents and a 1%-style listing pitch ([prnewswire.com](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/redfin-launches-in-canada-with-full-service-agents-in-toronto-and-national-home-search-site-300793765.html), Feb 2019; [cbc.ca](https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/redfin-canada-real-estate-1.5014763), 2019). It never expanded beyond BC/ON: **redfin.ca remains live as a search portal, with the brokerage licensed in British Columbia and Ontario and a "partner agent" program** (site pages current as of Sept 2025) ([redfin.ca](https://www.redfin.ca/), [redfin.ca/partners](https://www.redfin.ca/partners), accessed 2026-08-28). The widely-repeated claim that Redfin "exited Canada in 2022" could **not be verified** — the 2022 cuts were RedfinNow/US layoffs ([geekwire.com](https://www.geekwire.com/2022/redfin-to-cut-13-of-workforce-and-shut-down-home-flipping-business-redfinnow/), Nov 2022); the honest statement is that Redfin **de-emphasized Canada to a skeleton portal/partner operation** (UNVERIFIED beyond site evidence). Corporate context: **Rocket Companies completed its $1.75B all-stock acquisition of Redfin on 2025-07-01** ([ir.rocketcompanies.com](https://ir.rocketcompanies.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/Rocket-Companies-Completes-Acquisition-of-Redfin/default.aspx), Jul 2025), making any renewed Canadian investment even less likely (analysis).

**Net:** neither US giant is contesting owner-direct or Saskatchewan. The relevant US signal is product direction: Realtor.com's **"Search It, How You Say It"** natural-language search (Oct 2025) and **RealAssist AI** (June 2026, Gemini-based conversational search) ([housingwire.com](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/realtor-com-launches-ai-powered-home-search-tool/), Oct 2025; [googlecloudpresscorner.com](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-06-02-Realtor-com-R-Launches-RealAssist-TM-AI-A-Completely-Reimagined-Way-to-Find-A-Home), Jun 2026) — Portage's AI-chat search is on-trend, and incumbents will copy it; speed matters.

## Key takeaways for Portage

1. **The exact combination does not exist, but every component exists somewhere.** Owner-direct sales (Bōde, DuProprio, ComFree, PropertyGuys), free AVM (HonestDoor), AI valuation/search (HouseSigma; Realtor.com US), rental rails (liv.rent), room-sharing (SpacesShared) — Portage's novelty is the bundle + zero price + Regina focus, not any single feature (§1, §2, §5).
2. **Bōde is the company to beat/study**: alive, $949-or-1%-capped, already covering Saskatchewan, syndicating to MLS/Zillow ([bode.ca](https://bode.ca/), accessed 2026-08-28). Portage's "free" undercuts it only if Portage can live without its success fee.
3. **"Free for everyone" has no Canadian precedent at scale** — the nearest claimant (OpenHaus) has no verified traction. Every funded attempt to shrink fees below cost died in a downturn (FairSquare 2023, Properly 2023, Unreserved 2023) (§1.5, §1.11, §1.12).
4. **The FairSquare autopsy is the core warning:** fixed/zero fee revenue + cyclical volume + human-service costs = death, even with Desjardins' backing ([theglobeandmail.com](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-desjardins-group-shuts-down-fairsquare-realty-blames-slow-housing/), 2023). Portage must keep marginal cost per listing near zero (AI self-serve, no salaried agents) and monetize the home-services marketplace from day one.
5. **DuProprio proves owner-direct can take ~20% of a market** — but its enablers (notary conveyancing, 25-year brand, parent funding) don't transfer automatically to Saskatchewan (§3). SK's practical equivalent: cheap lawyers + a trusted local brand; Portage should bundle discounted legal/conveyancing partners the way DuProprio bundles notaries.
6. **MLS access is the strategic fork.** Off-MLS generalists outside Quebec have always starved; mere postings cost $500-$1,500 via brokerages (§6). Options: partner with a flat-fee SK brokerage, hold a brokerage licence in a silo, or deliberately build off-MLS liquidity in one city. Unreserved shows the industry will litigate/obstruct aggressive entrants (§1.12).
7. **Saskatchewan is genuinely underserved:** HouseSigma absent, Point2 (Saskatoon's own) exited for-sale listings in 2024, liv.rent absent, Wahi/Bōde nominal — while Regina posts record sales with 1.6 months supply (§1.6, §1.8, §7). A focused local player faces weak *direct* tech competition, mainly Realtor.ca + Facebook/Kijiji.
8. **Rental-side gap in Regina is the fastest wedge:** RentFaster charges $54.50/listing and leads the market; free tiers (Kijiji/Facebook) are scam-ridden — half of US rental-scam reports start on Facebook ([FTC](https://search.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/rental-scams-spotlight-2025.pdf), 2025). Portage's verified-owner ID + free listings directly attacks both incumbents' weaknesses (§2).
9. **Trust/verification is the defensible feature, not AI.** liv.rent monetizes Equifax-backed Trust Scores; scams are the #1 renter pain. Portage's ID validation + 24h moderation is the right bet; AI chat search will be commoditized (Realtor.com already shipped it US-wide, June 2026) (§2, §4).
10. **AVM is table stakes:** HonestDoor gives free valuations nationally, incl. SK, since 2022 (§1.10). "Portage estimate" must differentiate on local open-data depth (Regina assessments, neighbourhood scores), not existence.
11. **Room-type filters (entire/private/shared) are validated by others' traction:** SpacesShared already operates in Saskatchewan with 10K+ users via college partnerships (§5). Partnering with U of R / Sask Polytech would mirror their proven student channel.
12. **Investor climate is hostile to marketplaces, friendly to AI:** 54% of 2025 Canadian VC went to AI; marketplace/transaction-fee models are out of favour ([betakit.com](https://betakit.com/how-ai-and-a-tight-fundraising-market-are-reshaping-canadian-proptech/)). Pitch Portage as AI-native housing infrastructure with services revenue, not "a free portal."
13. **Incumbent risk is rising, not falling:** REALTOR.ca went for-profit (Jan 2025) under an ex-Kijiji CEO (Jan 2026) — expect faster feature copying and possible consumer-facing monetization (§1.1). Zillow/Redfin are dormant in Canada and unlikely to contest Regina (§4).
14. **Watch ComFree's relaunch trajectory** — $497-$1,197 flat-fee national FSBO with the strongest legacy brand in English-Canada FSBO; if it gains traction in the Prairies it occupies Portage's sell-side narrative first (§3).
15. **Monetization menu proven by others (for later, without breaking "free"):** featured placement (DuProprio/Rentals.ca), screening reports (liv.rent), services marketplace referrals (OpenHaus's stated model), data products (HonestDoor), mortgage/insurance referrals (Zolo/Wahi) (§1-2).

## Verdict: does this already exist?

**No single company is Portage.** As of 2026-08-28 there is no Canadian platform that is simultaneously (a) owner-direct for both sales and rentals, (b) genuinely zero-fee for everyone, (c) AI-native in search and listing creation, and (d) present in Regina. The nearest analogs are **Bōde** (owner-direct sales, success-fee, SK-registered, alive), **DuProprio** (the model's proof-of-concept, Quebec-only), the **relaunched ComFree** (flat-fee national FSBO, 2024-), and **OpenHaus** (claims zero-commission/free; unproven). The rental bundle Portage proposes exists only at liv.rent (paid, no Regina) and RentFaster (paid, no AI/verification depth).

However, the graveyard is instructive: Purplebricks/FairSquare (~$60M+ deployed), Properly, and Unreserved ($33.85M seed) all died or gutted their models between 2022-2023 attacking commissions with venture/corporate money. What has never been tried is Portage's specific configuration — near-zero marginal cost AI self-serve, services-marketplace monetization, and a single small underserved city as a beachhead. That configuration is unproven, not occupied. The honest conclusion: **the space is open in Regina, the bundle is novel, and the business-model risk (not the competition) is the primary threat.** Unverified residuals worth founder diligence: OpenHaus's traction, Bōde's SK listing depth, Unreserved's current state, DuProprio's current market share, and any unannounced REALTOR.ca AI roadmap.
